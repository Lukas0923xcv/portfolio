About me

I'm doing my apprenticeship as an Informatiker EFZ in platform development at the Technische Fachschule Bern, class INF2025, where both school and practical training take place. In the practical part I mostly work on networks, including a high-availability pfSense firewall cluster.

Since 2025 I've run a homelab at home. Most of what I know about running services, I learned by breaking it and fixing it again.

I'm not an application developer. When a project needs code, I write it with AI. I decide what the solution has to do, check and test the result, and run it myself.
