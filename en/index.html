  1. 01

    Netzwächter

    ESP32 · LTE · Flutter

    SMS alarm when the power goes out: a LilyGO T-A7670G with a prepaid SIM, its own firmware and an Android app.

    Hardware · personal
    Details: Netzwächter
    why
    While my grandparents were on holiday, their freezer failed and nobody noticed. One SMS at the right moment would have been enough.
    how
    An ESP32 with an LTE modem and an 18650 battery. The firmware notices when the power supply stops delivering, and sends an alarm and an all-clear to up to five numbers. More than 20 SMS commands control it remotely, and the Android app shows its status over Bluetooth.
    my part
    Hardware, wiring and testing. I wrote the firmware and app with AI and tested every version on the real hardware.
    status
    SMS alarm and app work (app v1.5.0).

  2. 02

    Passkey login for pfSense

    PHP · WebAuthn

    A second factor for the pfSense web interface: after the password, a passkey. Built in one day with AI, open source.

    Security · personal
    Details: Passkey login for pfSense
    why
    The login to my firewall only asked for a password, and pfSense offers no passkeys for its web interface.
    how
    An add-on that asks for a passkey after the password. The signature is checked entirely on the firewall, with no outside service. An emergency way in from trusted networks keeps me from locking myself out, and the hooks survive pfSense updates.
    my part
    I decided what the add-on may do, for example that it only applies once two passkeys are registered, and tested it. I wrote the code with AI.
    status
    Version 1.0.1, open source (Apache 2.0).

    Read the full case study

  3. 03

    Homelab proxmox-01

    Proxmox · OPNsense · TrueNAS · Ansible

    A Proxmox server I run like a small company network: separate network zones, single sign-on, automated updates and off-site backups.

    Platform · personal
    Details: Homelab proxmox-01
    why
    Started in 2025 out of curiosity, as a place to try things. Today family and friends use its services every day, so it has to be reliable.
    how
    A Proxmox host with TrueNAS storage (four disks in RAIDZ1). Public services run in a separate DMZ behind a reverse proxy, Authentik and CrowdSec. Updates run through Ansible, Pulse watches everything, and backups go to storage daily and off-site, encrypted, every week.

    Running daily. Since November 2026 a dedicated OPNsense handles the firewall.

  6. 06

    OPNsense firewall

    OPNsense · VLAN · DMZ

    A dedicated OPNsense for the homelab with separate networks for LAN and DMZ. Replaced the virtual pfSense in November 2026.

    Network · personal
    Details: OPNsense firewall
    why
    Until now the firewall ran as a virtual pfSense on the Proxmox host. Now a dedicated OPNsense does the job.
    how
    LAN and DMZ arrive as VLANs over a trunk, and the existing subnets stayed the same so the move needed no changes to the services. The gateways sit on a virtual IP from the start, so a second device for high availability can be added later.
    status
    Since November 2026 the whole network runs through the OPNsense, the pfSense is switched off.
    next
    A second factor for the web interface, and a separate network for IoT devices that may only reach the internet.
