Imprint and privacy

Who runs this site

Lukas Dalloz, Switzerland. This is a private, non-commercial website. You can reach me by e-mail: lukas.dalloz [at] proton.me

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers: fonts, images and scripts all come from my own server. There is no analytics service and no advertising.

Your choice of light or dark design is saved in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to me.

Server logs

Like every web server, mine writes a log for each request: IP address, time, the page requested, the browser's user agent and the referring page. I use these logs only to find errors and to protect the server against attacks, for example with CrowdSec, and delete them regularly. They are not passed on.

E-mail

If you write to me, I use your address and message only to reply.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection you can ask me what data about you I have, and have it corrected or deleted. Just write me an e-mail.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub and other sites open those sites. Their own privacy rules apply there.
